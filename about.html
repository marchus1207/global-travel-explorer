About Our Project

Global Travel Explorer was created to solve a common problem faced by travelers worldwide: finding comprehensive, location-specific travel information all in one place. We're starting with Norway and will expand to cover destinations across the globe.

Our interactive map brings together everything travelers need - from discovering amazing attractions to finding nearby accommodations, restaurants, hiking trails, and transportation options.

Our Mission

We aim to create the most comprehensive travel planning tool for destinations around the world, helping travelers discover the perfect places to stay, eat, and explore near every attraction. By integrating booking capabilities directly into our interactive map, we eliminate the need to juggle multiple websites and apps when planning adventures anywhere.

Future Development

We're actively working on enhancing our platform with:
